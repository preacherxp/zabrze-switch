## Composition recommendation

These are design suggestions, not geographical claims. Allocate separate silhouettes across the 320 × 150 visible logical area: Carnall around x=25–80, tower around x=105–153, compact DMiT around x=169–226, Arena around x=234–315. Keep the tower highest and leave sky gaps between the two verticals. Compress the stadium vertically; retain its light cage pattern. Reserve a clear fascia for short names rather than attempting every full official name.

Use a contemporary pedestrian foreground with visitors, a cyclist and a red-and-cream tram on a separate lower track strip. Trams are grounded in the city’s present transport, especially the Wolności corridor; the city’s 2025 report describes their continuing role. [Official city report for 2025](https://bip.miastozabrze.pl/attachment/99699). Treat this as a city-wide collage, not a claim that a tram runs in front of all four buildings. Avoid route numbers and unverified future rolling stock. Street lamps, lit museum windows, DMiT entrance lighting and the stadium concourse can provide separate dusk/night rhythms; exact decorative lighting schedules are artistic choices.

## Current-state limits

The Arena homepage currently gives 28,236 places, while its stadium subpage also retains older 24,563-place wording. The city’s 2025 report describes phased opening and unfinished functional areas of the fourth stand. Therefore omit capacity, claims of complete construction and any proposed final-state rendering. The museum’s live site advertises November–December 2026 visits/events, supporting the present visitor interpretation of its heritage sites. [Museum live site](https://sztolnialuiza.pl/).
